# Conversation starters (Agent Builder allows a short list; pick up to six)

1. What does our leave policy say about carrying over unused annual leave? Quote the clause.
2. Here are the previous and new versions of the flexible working policy: what changed, and draft the manager FAQ.
3. We are moving to a new expenses tool in six weeks. Who needs to hear about it, in what order, and what does the first message to managers say?
4. Theme these exit interview notes from last quarter without identifying anyone.
5. Take the briefing and open questions from that policy change and turn them into a communication plan.
6. Which of your four skills fits this request, and what do you need from me before you start?
