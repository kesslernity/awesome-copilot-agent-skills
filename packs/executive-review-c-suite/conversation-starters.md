# Conversation starters (Agent Builder allows a short list; pick up to six)

1. Run a CFO review of this business case: pressure-test the payback and the run costs.
2. What would a COO say about the timeline and capacity in this delivery plan?
3. Rehearse the technology seat: challenge the architecture and vendor choices in this proposal.
4. Check the security, privacy and third-party risk in this plan the way a CISO would.
5. Pressure-test the people impact of this restructuring paper before the people committee.
6. Which seats should review this deck before the board, and in what order?
