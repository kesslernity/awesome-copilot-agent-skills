# Conversation starters (Agent Builder allows a short list; pick up to six)

1. Run a CDO review on this business case: are the metric definitions and data sources sound?
2. What would legal say about this partnership proposal before the deal committee sees it?
3. Pressure-test the capital case in this deck the way an investment committee would.
4. Review this vendor renewal commercially, as a Head of Procurement would, and check the exit terms.
5. Give me the shop-floor view of this rollout plan: will the team work with it or around it?
6. Run the full bench on this plan and finish with a summary table of the verdicts.
