# Rating and stance rules

Read at procedure steps 5 to 8, 10 and 12 of the stakeholder map builder. A house scale or vocabulary supplied by the user replaces the defaults below and is named in the header.

## Fragment rules
- A fragment is a verbatim copy of the stakeholder's own words, up to 25 words, with source code, location and date.
- Prefer the most recent fragment. An earlier fragment with a different classification goes in the Trend column.
- Paraphrase is not a fragment. Minutes that summarise ("the finance lead raised concerns") are quoted as written and labelled "minutes wording, not direct speech".
- A third party's account of someone's view is a fragment for the third party's own concern, never for the person described.
- Silence, absence from a meeting, a late reply or a short reply is not evidence of any stance or interest.

## Interest rating bases (default High, Medium, Low, UNKNOWN)
Write the basis in the row, quoting or citing the source. Use the strongest basis that the evidence supports.
- High: the stakeholder states a want, fear or requirement about the project in their own words; or the sources show the project changes their team's work, budget, headcount or systems directly; or they raise the project in three or more separate sources.
- Medium: the sources show an indirect effect on their work, or they raise the project in one or two sources without a stated want or fear.
- Low: the sources show them informed only, with no stated position and no effect on their work shown.
- UNKNOWN: no source shows a stated position or an effect. UNKNOWN is never rounded down to Low.

## Influence rating bases (default High, Medium, Low, UNKNOWN)
Test in this order and stop at the first basis with evidence. Quote the governing text.
1. Decision or veto right over the project or a stage gate, quoted from the charter, terms of reference or RACI (Accountable, Approver): High.
2. Control of budget, people or systems the project needs, as a source states it: High.
3. Named escalation role or steering member without a decision right stated: Medium.
4. Consulted in the RACI or named as a subject matter reference: Medium.
5. Informed only: Low.
6. No evidence on any of the above: UNKNOWN. Job title, grade, seniority or reputation is never a basis on its own.

## Stance vocabulary and signal phrases (default)
Classify only with these labels. Signal phrases guide the reading; the fragment decides.
- Supportive: "we need this", "fully behind", "let us go ahead", "my team is ready", a stated commitment of people or budget.
- Neutral: "no objection", "fine either way", "noted", a stated position with no preference.
- Concerned: "my concern is", "not comfortable with", "what happens if", "before we proceed I would want", a condition attached to support.
- Opposed: "I do not support", "we should not", "this will not work for us", a stated request to stop, defer or cancel.
- Mixed: fragments from the same period that fall under two labels; both are quoted.
- UNKNOWN: no fragment.

## Grid labels (engagement effort, never the person)
Interest and influence both rated:
- High influence, High interest: "engage closely".
- High influence, Medium or Low interest: "keep satisfied".
- Medium or Low influence, High interest: "keep informed".
- Medium influence, Medium interest: "engage at governance cadence".
- Medium influence, Low interest: "monitor".
- Low influence, Medium or Low interest: "monitor".
Any axis UNKNOWN: "unplaced". Grid labels are engagement effort words; they are not descriptions of the person and never appear beside a name without the ratings that produced them.

## Engagement plan fields (every row labelled "proposed")
- Objective: what the project needs from this stakeholder or owes to them, per the sources (a decision at a gate, a resource release, an update owed under the communication plan).
- Channel: one the sources name for that person or forum; otherwise one from the default set: one-to-one, governance forum and written update.
- Cadence: as the communication plan or terms of reference state; otherwise "to be set".
- Proposed owner: a role the sources name (project manager, sponsor, workstream lead); never a person the sources do not name in a project role.
- Next touch: a proposed step, not a booked one.
- Concern or question to raise: the stakeholder's own fragment, quoted.

## Banned descriptors
Never appear in the agent's own text, in any table or in the closing report, even when a source uses them. Where a source uses one, quote the fragment inside the Stance table only, with the source code.
ally, enemy, champion, blocker, detractor, saboteur, gatekeeper, troublemaker, difficult, obstructive, resistant, hostile, political, passive, aggressive, defensive, ambitious, ego, agenda, hidden agenda, unreliable, weak, lazy, toxic, negative, loyal, on side, against us.

## Sensitivity pass checklist
- Health, family, leave, personal circumstances: removed.
- Performance, competence, promotion or disciplinary remarks: removed.
- Banned descriptors in the agent's own text: struck.
- Every stance cell is a fragment with source and date, or UNKNOWN.
- Header carries the restricted circulation marker.
