# Persona brief template and reaction vocabulary

Used by the communication-pretest-panel skill. Personas are roles the user describes. The agent fills only what the user supplies; every other field is UNKNOWN and lowers confidence for the reactions that depend on it.

## Persona brief fields

| Field | What to capture | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| Persona label | Short role label used in every table (for example "Site supervisor", "New joiner", "Key account customer") | Required; ask |
| Role and daily work | What this person does and where the message reaches them (desk, floor, mobile, inbox volume) | UNKNOWN; reactions on channel fit are low confidence |
| Cares about | The two or three things this role protects: time, headcount, budget, safety of the crew, customer promises, career | UNKNOWN; objections are low confidence |
| Knows already | What this role has been told about the topic before this message, and by whom | UNKNOWN; questions are low confidence |
| Stake | What the change costs or gives this role, in their own terms | UNKNOWN; first reaction is low confidence |
| History | How similar messages were received before (ignored, resented, welcomed, worked around) | UNKNOWN; next-step is low confidence |
| Tone expected | Formal, plain, direct, warm; what reads as condescending or evasive to this role | UNKNOWN; misreadings are low confidence |
| Relationship to sender | Reports to, peer, customer, unknown to them | UNKNOWN |
| Reads in | Language and reading time available | Default: the message's language, two minutes |

## First-reaction vocabulary

Choose one primary term and, if needed, one secondary. Always cite the trigger passage.

relief, agreement, interest, indifference, confusion, scepticism, irritation, worry, alarm, distrust, feeling blamed, feeling excluded, feeling talked down to, fatigue (another message on the same topic).

## Objection types

| Type | Typical wording from the persona |
|---|---|
| Timing | "Why now, and why so late" |
| Burden | "This adds work and nobody removed anything" |
| Fairness | "Why us and not them" |
| Credibility | "We were told the opposite in <earlier message>" |
| Competence | "Whoever wrote this has not seen how we work" |
| Vagueness | "What exactly changes on Monday" |
| Consultation | "Nobody asked us" |

## Question types

what happens to me, what do I have to do, by when, who decides, who do I ask, what if I cannot, what happens if I do nothing, where is the detail, is this final.

## Next-step vocabulary

acts as asked, ignores, forwards to peers, asks line manager, asks the sender, escalates to a representative body, works around, waits for the follow-up.

## Confidence

High: all fields that the reaction depends on were supplied. Low: any dependent field UNKNOWN. State the field in the row.
