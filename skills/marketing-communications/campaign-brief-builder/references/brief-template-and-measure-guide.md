# Brief template and measure guide

Used by the campaign brief builder at Procedure steps 3, 6, 7, 9 and 11. Defaults apply only where the inputs are silent; an organisation's own brief template overrides the section order, and the override is noted in the header.

## Default section order and word budget

The one-page target is 450 to 600 words of body text and table cells, excluding the header, the UNKNOWN list and the embedded instructions line. Where a section runs over, cut detail into an annex titled "Annex: supporting detail" and leave one line in the body pointing to it.

| Section | Budget (words) | What it must contain |
|---|---|---|
| Objectives | 40 to 60 | Two traced lines, business and marketing, each tested |
| Audience | 80 to 120 | Primary audience; secondary only where the material names one |
| Message | 60 to 100 | One proposition, up to three supporting messages with proof |
| Channels and timeline | 60 to 90 | One row per channel, phases with dates or relative weeks |
| Success measures | 60 to 90 | One to three measures per objective |
| Mandatories and exclusions | 30 to 60 | Quoted only |
| Risks | 40 to 60 | Sourced or gap-derived, each with an owner |
| Open questions | as needed | Never cut to fit; move other detail to the annex first |

## Objective test

Each objective is tested against four questions. An objective is "Measurable: yes" only when all four have an answer in the sources.

| Question | Passes when | When it fails |
|---|---|---|
| What changes? | A named behaviour, belief, volume or value | Open question: "What should this campaign change?" |
| For whom? | An audience the audience material describes | Open question naming the gap |
| By how much? | A quantity or a direction with a baseline in the sources | Open question; never a placeholder figure |
| By when? | A date or period in the constraints | Open question; relative weeks in the timeline |

## Channel roles

Roles describe what a channel is asked to do in this campaign. The reason column must quote or cite the input that supports the choice (for example an audience note saying where the audience already is).

| Role | Meaning | Typical evidence in the inputs |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Put the message in front of the audience for the first time | Audience material naming where the audience spends attention |
| Engage | Hold attention and build understanding | Channel notes on formats that allow depth |
| Convert | Move the audience to the desired action | The action named in the marketing objective and where it can be taken |
| Retain | Keep an existing audience acting or believing | Customer or member lists the organisation already owns |

A channel with no supporting evidence is listed with role "(default, confirm)" and a confirmation item.

## Measure guide

Measures follow the objective. The guide lists families to choose from; the agent writes the measure only when the data source exists in the inputs or the user names one. No target is proposed without a stated baseline.

| Objective type | Measure families | Baseline needed | Typical data source (as stated by the user) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach, impressions, unique visitors, recall as reported by research the user supplies | Current reach or visitors for the same audience | Analytics export, platform statistics, research report |
| Consideration | Content views, time on page, downloads, sign-ups, enquiries opened | Current monthly figure | Analytics, form tool, mailing platform |
| Conversion | Purchases, applications, bookings, qualified leads, trial starts | Current monthly figure and conversion rate | Sales system, commerce platform, registration system |
| Retention | Repeat purchase, renewal rate, active users, unsubscribe rate | Current rate over a stated period | Customer system, subscription platform |
| Internal or behavioural | Adoption of a process, attendance, completion rate | Current rate | Learning system, attendance records, survey the user supplies |

Column rules:
- Baseline: the figure as stated with its date, or UNKNOWN.
- Target: as stated by the user, or "target: to set". A direction ("increase") without a figure is recorded as the direction plus "figure: to set".
- Read date: when the measure will be read, from the timeline; absent, "end of campaign plus <interval the user states>" or UNKNOWN.

## Open question conventions

- One question per row, phrased so a yes, a number, a name or a date answers it.
- "Best answered by" names a role from the inputs (campaign owner, approver, analyst, legal reviewer); a role the inputs do not name reads "role: UNKNOWN".
- "Blocks" names the section or decision that cannot close until the answer arrives.
- Questions are numbered in order of what they block: objectives first, then measures, message, channels, timeline.

## Words that do not appear in the brief

Unless quoted from a source with its reference: best, leading, first, only, guaranteed, proven, number one, market leader, award-winning, fastest, cheapest, safest, and any comparison against a named competitor. The message section states what the offer does in the sources' own words.
