# Announcement structures and approval checklist

Used by the announcement drafter at Procedure steps 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9. Defaults apply only where the inputs are silent; the organisation's own style guide and approval matrix override every table here, and the override is noted in the header.

## Reader questions

Every announcement is checked against these seven questions in this order. A question with no fact in the sources reads UNKNOWN in the Reader questions table and becomes an open question.

| Number | Question | Where it normally sits |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is happening? | First sentence |
| 2 | When? | First or second sentence, with time zone |
| 3 | Why? | Second paragraph |
| 4 | What changes for me? | Third paragraph |
| 5 | What must I do, and by when? | Action paragraph, one action per line |
| 6 | Where do I get help or more information? | Before the sign-off |
| 7 | Who is telling me? | Sign-off, name and role |

## Structures by type and audience

| Type | Audience | Order of parts |
|---|---|---|
| Change (system, process, policy, organisation) | Internal | News, effective date, why, what changes for the reader, action and date, support route, sign-off |
| People news (appointment, departure, move) | Internal | News, effective date, role and scope, one line of background as stated, what changes for the reader, sign-off. No reason for a departure unless the source states it and records the person's agreement |
| Programme or initiative launch | Internal | News, purpose in one sentence, who is involved, what happens first, how to take part, support route, sign-off |
| Customer or partner notice | External | News, effective date, what changes for the recipient, what the recipient must do, support route, sign-off |
| Press statement | External | Headline, dateline, news in one sentence, two or three fact paragraphs, attributed quote only if supplied, boilerplate as supplied, media contact as supplied |
| Holding statement (incident) | Either | What is known with its time, what is being done as stated by the accountable function, what is not yet known, when the next update comes, contact. Nothing else |
| Result or milestone | Either | News with the figure and its source, context as stated, what it means for the reader, thanks only where the sender asks, sign-off |

## Length budgets by channel

Budgets count body words, excluding the subject line, header and sign-off. The user's figure overrides.

| Channel | Body words | Subject or headline |
|---|---|---|
| Email | 150 to 300 | Under 60 characters |
| Intranet post | 200 to 400 | Under 70 characters |
| Town hall script | 250 to 450 (about three minutes spoken) | Title only |
| Customer or partner notice | 150 to 300 | Under 60 characters |
| Press statement | 250 to 450 | Under 90 characters |
| Social post | 40 to 80 | None |

## Neutral default voice

Applies only when no style guide or sample is supplied, and is marked "(default voice, confirm)".
- First person plural for the organisation ("we"), second person for the reader ("you").
- Sentences under 25 words; one idea per sentence; active voice.
- Plain words: use, start, end, change, help. No jargon the audience material does not use.
- No exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions, none of the words listed under "Words that do not appear".
- Sign-off: name, role, date.

## Standard approval set

Used when the inputs name no approvers or approval matrix. Every row is "(default, confirm)"; the agent never marks a status.

| Trigger | Approver role | What they check |
|---|---|---|
| Every announcement | Sender | Facts, tone, timing, and that they are willing to sign it |
| Every announcement | Communications lead | Voice, structure, channel fit, sequencing |
| Affects employment, pay, roles, locations or personal data | Human resources lead | Employment law wording, consultation obligations, personal data |
| Legal, regulatory, contractual, incident, or any external audience | Legal counsel | Admissions, forward-looking statements, regulatory notification duties |
| Financial figures or results | Finance lead | Figures against the approved source, disclosure timing |
| Safety, health or environmental incident | Health, safety and environment lead | Facts against the incident record; that no statement of cause, blame, safety or resumption appears |
| Security incident or system change | Information security or technology lead | Technical facts, what customers or staff must do |
| Named third party (customer, partner, supplier) | That party's named contact, as stated | Consent to be named and to any quote |
| Translation planned | Reviewer fluent in the target language | Meaning preserved, terms as in the glossary |

## Sensitive topic rules

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Personal data | No name, health detail, family detail or reason for absence unless the source states the person's consent |
| Redundancy, restructuring, closure | State what is decided, what is proposed and what is under consultation exactly as the source distinguishes them; never collapse "proposed" into "decided" |
| Safety, health, environmental or security incident | Facts with times as recorded; no cause, no blame, no "no one was at fault", no "the area is safe", no "operations will resume", no "systems are secure" unless the source states it, and then attributed to the accountable function |
| Financial results | Figures only as in the approved source; no rounding, no forecast not in the source; disclosure timing is an approver check |
| Legal or regulatory | No admission, no apology that assigns fault, no characterisation of a counterparty; counsel row required |
| Health | No medical advice; point to the source the organisation names |

## Pre-send checks

| Check | Passes when |
|---|---|
| News first | The first sentence states what is happening |
| Dates | Every date has a day, month and year, and a time zone where a time is given |
| Names and titles | Spelt exactly as in the source |
| Links and attachments | Listed as named placeholders ("[link: policy page]"); none invented |
| Help route | A contact, mailbox, channel or page is named from the source, or reads [UNKNOWN: help route] |
| Length | Body words within the channel budget |
| Subject line | Within the channel character limit and stating the news, not a teaser |
| Accessibility | Alt text placeholder for each planned image; no meaning carried by colour alone |
| Sequencing | Internal audience hears before or with external; managers before their teams where the inputs ask for it |
| Reply handling | Who answers replies is named or reads UNKNOWN |

## Words that do not appear

Unless quoted from a source with its reference: exciting, thrilled, delighted, journey, synergy, world-class, best-in-class, leading, seamless, robust, unfortunately, regrettably, going forward, reach out, circle back, at this time, safe, secure, resolved, fixed, no impact, business as usual.
