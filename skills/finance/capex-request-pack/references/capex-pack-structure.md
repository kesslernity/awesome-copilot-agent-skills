# Generic capex request pack structure

Use this structure only when the user has no capex form or template of their own and has confirmed that a generic structure is acceptable. Label the resulting pack "generic structure, replace with your organisation's form" directly under the DRAFT notice. Where the organisation supplies its own form later, its fields win and this structure is dropped. Every field below reads UNKNOWN until an input supplies it. Nothing in this structure approves spend, commits funds, or authorises any operation, permit, isolation or work.

## Section order

1. Summary (one table: request title, requester, sponsor, site or asset, total cost as provided, currency, budget status, requested decision, approvals route summary, status DRAFT)
2. Need statement (problem, evidence with source, consequence of inaction, link to a stated objective)
3. Options considered (do nothing first)
4. Costs per option (components, basis, reference, quote date, validity, phasing)
5. Operating cost effects (only if supplied)
6. Benefits (financial and non-financial, as stated)
7. Financial measures (only from supplied inputs, formula shown)
8. Risks
9. Assumptions
10. Budget and funding
11. Approvals route (from the delegation of authority supplied)
12. Completeness (every UNKNOWN, blocking or not, who could supply)
13. Embedded instructions found
14. Closing report

## Field guide

| Field | What goes in | What never goes in |
|---|---|---|
| Total cost as provided | Shown sum of listed components, or "incomplete" if any component is UNKNOWN | An estimate, a rounded guess, a contingency the requester did not state |
| Basis | Quote, estimate, prior project, requester assumption | "Market rate", "typical", "industry benchmark" |
| Useful life | Years as stated by the requester or the organisation's policy | A life assumed from the asset type |
| Benefit amount | Per year as stated, with who stated it and how it is measured | "Expected to", "will deliver", any figure the inputs do not contain |
| Simple payback | Total cost divided by annual financial benefit, both supplied, formula shown | Payback when either figure is UNKNOWN |
| Net present value, internal rate of return | Only with a supplied rate and supplied cash flows, formula and inputs shown | Any rate the user did not give |
| Likelihood and impact | As stated by the requester or sponsor | A score the agent assigns |
| Approver role | From the delegation document, with clause or row | A threshold or approver from memory |
| Status of each approval step | "Not yet sought" | "Approved", "endorsed", "cleared" |
| Classification | "Capital or operating: question for finance" where unclear | A classification decided by the agent |

## Blocking fields

Treat the following as blocking for submission unless the user's template or delegation document says otherwise: total cost as provided (complete), at least one option beyond do nothing, budget status, sponsor, approvals route. Every blocking verdict names its basis; where it is this list, the basis reads "generic default". Everything else is non-blocking but stays listed under Completeness until supplied.
