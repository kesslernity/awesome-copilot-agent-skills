# Close task families (suggestion list only)

Use this list in two ways only: (a) to spot task families that a close calendar appears to lack, reported in the "Suggested additions for the controller" table; (b) as the generic register when the user has no close calendar and has typed a go-ahead to proceed with it. In either case the controller accepts, changes or rejects every line. The list never replaces the organisation's own calendar, never carries due days of its own, and nothing in it authorises any posting, approval, operation, permit, isolation or work.

Conventions: WD means working day after period end, per the convention stated in the checklist. "Typical predecessor" is a common sequence, not a rule; the calendar's own predecessors always win. Owner and due WD are UNKNOWN until the controller supplies them.

| Family | Typical tasks | Typical predecessor | Common evidence of completion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cut-off and sub-ledger close | Stop goods receipt and invoice posting for the period; close purchase, sales, inventory and payroll sub-ledgers; run period-end interfaces | None (first in sequence) | Sub-ledger close log or system status |
| Cash and bank | Post all bank transactions; clear unmatched receipts and payments; bank reconciliation per account | Sub-ledger close | Reconciliation with reviewer sign-off date |
| Accruals and prepayments | Book goods received not invoiced; expense accruals; release prepayments; reverse prior month accruals | Cut-off | Journal list with posting reference |
| Revenue and receivables | Recognise revenue per policy; unbilled revenue and deferred income; credit notes; receivables ageing; bad debt provision as instructed | Sales sub-ledger close | Revenue reconciliation to billing |
| Payables | Payables ageing; debit balances review; supplier statement reconciliations for key accounts | Purchase sub-ledger close | Ageing report and reconciliation |
| Payroll | Post payroll journal; reconcile payroll control accounts; accrue bonus and holiday as instructed | Payroll sub-ledger close | Control account reconciliation |
| Fixed assets | Capitalise additions from the clearing account; disposals; run depreciation; reconcile register to ledger | Payables close | Register to ledger reconciliation |
| Inventory | Post count adjustments; revalue as instructed; reconcile inventory sub-ledger to ledger | Inventory sub-ledger close | Reconciliation and adjustment journal |
| Intercompany | Match intercompany balances and transactions; agree differences; post agreed adjustments; confirm balances with counterparties | Sub-ledger closes on both sides | Matching report with residual differences listed |
| Tax | Indirect tax reconciliation; current tax accrual per instruction; deferred tax as instructed | Ledger substantially complete | Reconciliation to return or computation |
| Balance sheet reconciliations | Every control and suspense account reconciled; reconciling items aged; suspense cleared or explained | Journals posted | Reconciliation pack with reviewer date |
| Foreign currency | Revalue foreign currency balances at period-end rates; post exchange differences | Balances complete | Revaluation report |
| Trial balance review | Analytical review against prior month and budget; unusual movements queried; late journals list | Reconciliations | Review notes with queries and answers |
| Consolidation | Submit entity trial balance; consolidation adjustments; eliminations; minority interests as instructed | Trial balance review | Submission confirmation |
| Reporting | Management accounts pack; variance commentary; flash results; disclosures as instructed | Consolidation or trial balance review | Pack issued with date |
| Post-close | Post-close review; late adjustment log; issues list for next month; calendar update | Reporting | Review notes and updated calendar |

Quarter-end and year-end families that many calendars add: impairment review inputs, provisions review, lease schedule refresh, going concern inputs, audit deliverables list, statutory disclosure schedules. Flag these as suggestions only when the period is a quarter or year end and the calendar does not name them.
