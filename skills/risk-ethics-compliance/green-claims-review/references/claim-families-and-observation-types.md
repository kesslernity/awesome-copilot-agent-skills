# Claim families and observation types

Default lists for the green claims review. The user may substitute a house list; the header of the review says which list was used.

## Claim families
| Family | Words and cues that place a claim here |
|---|---|
| General benefit | green, eco, eco-friendly, sustainable, planet-friendly, responsible, kind to nature |
| Climate | carbon neutral, net zero, climate positive, low carbon, carbon negative, offset, removal |
| Recyclability and recycled content | recyclable, made from recycled, X per cent recycled, closed loop |
| End of life | biodegradable, compostable, plastic-free, zero waste |
| Composition | natural, chemical-free, non-toxic, free from, organic |
| Efficiency | energy efficient, water saving, X per cent less energy |
| Packaging | reduced packaging, plastic-free packaging, refillable |
| Comparative | greener than, better for the planet than, X per cent less than |
| Future commitment | by a stated year, on track to, committed to |
| Labels and awards | certification marks, logos, seals, awards, scheme names |
| Implied | colour palettes, leaves, globes, animals, product or brand names containing eco or green, described imagery |

## Claim types
Absolute (a benefit stated without a comparator), comparative (against a named or unnamed alternative), future (a commitment or trajectory), label (a mark, logo or scheme name), implied (from imagery, colour or naming, recorded from the user's description).

## Observation types
Use exactly one per rules-mapping row. Where two types apply to the same claim and rule passage, add one row per type, repeating the claim ref and rule reference.
| Type | What was seen |
|---|---|
| Unqualified general claim | A general-benefit claim with no qualifier in the text |
| Comparator not named | A comparative claim whose basis of comparison the text does not state |
| Evidence scope narrower than claim | Evidence covers one product, site, year or boundary; the claim reaches wider |
| Evidence older than claim | Evidence dated before the product, process or period the claim describes |
| Future claim without stated plan | A commitment with no milestone, plan or interim figure in the text or evidence |
| Offset-based claim without stated disclosure | A neutral or net-zero claim resting on offsets where the supplied rule names a disclosure |
| Label without named scheme or with scope mismatch | A mark or logo with no scheme named, or a certificate whose stated scope does not include the claimed product |
| Figure without unit, baseline or boundary | A number with no unit, base year or boundary in text or evidence |
| Wording listed by the rule as needing qualification | The supplied rule names the term or phrase |
| Nothing further seen | Each element the supplied passage names appears in the text or evidence as read; recorded for legal to confirm, not a clearance |

Each observation records what was seen against what the supplied rule asks for. It is not a finding of non-compliance and not a clearance; legal and marketing decide.
