# Plan structure and defaults

Used by the onboarding plan builder at Procedure steps 2, 7 and 8. Defaults apply only where the inputs are silent; anything the organisation's checklist, policy or template states overrides them, and the override is noted in the header.

## Phase outcomes: how to derive them

Each responsibility in the role document becomes up to three outcomes, one per phase, using these verbs. Keep the responsibility's own nouns; do not add scope.

| Phase | The new hire ... | Verbs to use |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 to 30: learn and connect | understands how the work is done here, has met the people the role depends on, has observed the process at least once, has working access | understands, has met, has observed, can find, has access to |
| Day 31 to 60: contribute with support | performs the responsibility on real work with review by the manager or a named colleague | performs with review, drafts, co-runs, completes a first ... |
| Day 61 to 90: own | performs the responsibility independently and reports on it at the Day 90 checkpoint | owns, runs, delivers, reports on |

A responsibility that the role document describes as occasional (annual, project-based) may have only a Day 30 outcome (understands the process, knows who to involve) and a note that the first real occurrence lies outside the plan.

## Week 1 default shape

Only where the checklist or template gives none. Every row is a proposal with the manager as default owner, marked "(default, confirm)".

| Day | Activity | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Welcome and plan walk-through with the manager | Agree how the plan will be used and where questions go |
| Day 1 | Equipment and access check against the request table | Find what is still missing and who is chasing it |
| Day 1 or 2 | Introduction to the buddy | A first point of contact for daily questions |
| Days 2 to 5 | Mandatory training exactly as the policy lists it | Policy requirement, as stated |
| Days 2 to 5 | Introductions to the people in the people map, two per day, within the Week 1 session cap in the load check | Understand roles and hand-offs |
| Day 5 | End of Week 1 checkpoint | See checkpoint agendas |

## Checkpoint agendas

Checkpoints are conversations about the plan. They describe what the hire can show; they do not rate the hire. Where the organisation's policy states a probation review, quote its wording and reference; never draft probation outcomes.

| Checkpoint | Attendees (default) | Agenda |
|---|---|---|
| End of Week 1 | Manager, new hire | Access and equipment gaps; people met and still to meet; questions collected; anything in the plan that already looks wrong |
| Day 30 | Manager, new hire (buddy optional) | Each Day 30 outcome: shown, partly shown, not yet, with what blocked it; adjust the Day 60 activities; confirm the plan still matches the role |
| Day 60 | Manager, new hire | Each Day 60 outcome; feedback in both directions; what support is still needed; check on load and pattern |
| Day 90 | Manager, new hire, plus anyone the policy names | Each Day 90 outcome; hand-over of remaining supported items to full ownership; what the hire needs next; close the plan or extend named items |

"What on track looks like" is written per checkpoint as the list of phase outcomes the hire can demonstrate, in the outcomes' own words. Words such as exceeds, underperforms, at risk, fails or passes do not appear.

## Load check defaults

| Measure | Default cap | Flag text |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled sessions per week (introductions, training, shadowing) | 10 in Week 1, 8 in Weeks 2 to 4, 5 thereafter | "Week N carries X sessions against a cap of Y; propose moving ... to Week M" |
| Sessions per day | 4 | "Day N carries X sessions; propose moving ..." |
| Activities owned by one person other than the hire in one week | 6 | "<Owner> carries X activities in Week N; propose reassigning ... or shifting to Week M" |
| Activities with no dependency satisfied | 0 | "Activity ... depends on access row ... which is needed by Day N; propose moving ..." |

Flags are listed in the load check table and in the confirmation list. Nothing is removed from the plan without the user's choice.

## Access table conventions

- "Needed by" is Day 1 for any system the role document or systems list says is used daily, otherwise the first activity that depends on it.
- "Status" is always "request to raise" in the draft. The plan never records that access was requested, approved or granted; the manager updates the status after the fact in their own copy.
- Approver or route not given: UNKNOWN, and a confirmation list row.
- Site access, badges, medicals, inductions and certifications are listed as requests with the policy reference only. The plan never states the hire is cleared, inducted or authorised for any site, system or task.
