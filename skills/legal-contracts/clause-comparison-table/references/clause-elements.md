# Clause families, elements, locating keywords and difference categories

The skill breaks a clause into the elements below so that each document fills one cell per element. Add elements when a document covers something the list does not; say so in the output. Elements are descriptive fields, never a checklist of what a clause should contain.

## Difference categories
One per differing cell.
- Scope: what the clause covers (subject matter, information, deliverables, losses).
- Party: who the clause binds or benefits; mutual or one-sided.
- Threshold or amount: a cap, floor, percentage, sum, number of days.
- Trigger or condition: the event or condition that starts, ends or excuses the obligation.
- Carve-out or exception: what is excluded from the general rule.
- Duration or timing: term, survival, notice period, deadline, time bar.
- Procedure: notice form, approval route, escalation, cure step.
- Defined term: the same word defined differently, or used undefined in one document.
- Silence: one document says nothing on the element.

## Families and elements

Limitation of liability. Keywords: liability, liable, cap, aggregate, consequential, indirect, exclude.
Elements: cap amount or basis; aggregate or per claim; period the cap refers to; mutual or one-sided; excluded loss types; carve-outs from the cap (fraud, wilful default, death or injury, confidentiality, data, indemnities, IP); time bar for claims; relationship to insurance.

Indemnities. Keywords: indemnify, hold harmless, defend, third-party claim.
Elements: indemnifying party; indemnified party; triggers; scope of losses; cap or uncapped; conduct-of-claims procedure; exclusions; survival.

Termination. Keywords: terminate, termination, breach, cure, insolvency, convenience.
Elements: for-cause grounds; cure period; for-convenience right and notice; insolvency trigger; change-of-control trigger; consequences (payment, return, transition); survival list.

Term and renewal. Keywords: term, commence, renew, extend, expiry.
Elements: start trigger; initial term; renewal mechanism (automatic, by notice, by agreement); notice period to prevent renewal; maximum term.

Confidentiality. Keywords: confidential, disclose, recipient, proprietary.
Elements: definition (marked, all, oral); exclusions; permitted purpose; permitted recipients; standard of care; compelled disclosure; duration; return or destruction; residuals; remedies.

Intellectual property. Keywords: intellectual property, licence, assign, background, foreground, deliverable.
Elements: ownership of background IP; ownership of deliverables or foreground IP; licence grant (scope, territory, duration, exclusivity, sublicensing); moral rights; third-party materials; open-source terms; improvements.

Payment. Keywords: fees, invoice, payment, due, interest, dispute.
Elements: payment terms in days; trigger (invoice, acceptance, milestone); late-payment interest; disputed-invoice procedure; set-off; taxes; currency; price adjustment.

Data protection. Keywords: personal data, controller, processor, processing, transfer, sub-processor.
Elements: roles; processing instructions; sub-processor approval; international transfer mechanism; security measures reference; breach notification period; audit; deletion or return.

Warranties. Keywords: warrant, represent, as is, disclaim.
Elements: warranties given by each party; duration; remedies; disclaimers.

Governing law and disputes. Keywords: governed, jurisdiction, courts, arbitration, mediation, escalation.
Elements: governing law; forum; arbitration seat and rules; escalation steps; injunctive relief carve-out.

Assignment and subcontracting. Keywords: assign, novate, subcontract, change of control.
Elements: consent requirement; group carve-out; change-of-control treatment; subcontracting consent; liability for subcontractors.

Force majeure. Keywords: force majeure, beyond reasonable control, event.
Elements: definition and listed events; exclusions (payment, labour disputes); notice; mitigation; termination right after a period.

Service levels. Keywords: service level, availability, credit, response, resolution.
Elements: metrics and targets; measurement period; credits; cap on credits; sole-remedy wording; termination trigger; reporting.

Non-solicitation and exclusivity. Keywords: solicit, hire, exclusive, standstill.
Elements: who is covered; duration; territory; exceptions (general advertising); remedy.

## Notes
- A family not listed here: build elements from the baseline document's own structure and label them "derived from baseline".
- Governing law differs between documents: record it per document; the comparison is of wording only.
- Every element value is quoted or marked Silent. No cell is filled by inference.
