# Decision memo template and extraction guide

Use this layout only when the user has no house template and has typed a go-ahead for a generic one. Label the memo "generic layout, replace with your organisation's template" directly under the DRAFT notice. When a house template arrives later, its fields win. Every field reads UNKNOWN until a fragment or the user's explicit input supplies it. Nothing in this template ratifies a decision or authorises any operation, permit, isolation or work.

## One-page layout

| Field | Source | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Memo reference | Decision register convention, else DM-<YYYY-MM-DD>-01 | A reference that implies the memo is already logged |
| Forum and discussion date | User input or the source header | Today's date in place of the discussion date |
| Decision-maker or body | Named in the discussion or by the user | Assumed from the most senior participant |
| Decision | The discussion's own words, then the verbatim fragment and location | Firmer wording than was spoken |
| Status | Decided; decided subject to condition; deferred to date; escalated to body; not taken | Any sixth state, or a status the fragment does not support |
| Owner | The person named as accountable for carrying the decision | The chair by default, the note-taker, the loudest speaker |
| Effective date | As stated, resolved against the discussion date | A date the agent picks |
| Review date or trigger | As stated; a trigger where no date was given | A default review interval |
| Options considered | Every alternative raised, with who raised it and the stated reason | An option nobody raised, marked rejected |
| Evidence cited | What participants invoked, attached or mentioned, figures as spoken | Evidence the agent looked up or remembers |
| Dissent recorded | Every objection with speaker, fragment, answered status, recorded-at-request flag | "Some concerns were raised" |
| Conditions and open points | Every condition quoted in the status, every question not framed as an objection, every unresolved point, each with who raised it, resolved status, owner as stated and fragment | An item without a fragment; an owner assumed |
| Authority | Clause quoted verbatim from supplied terms of reference or delegation, labelled "candidate clause, for the user to confirm"; no matching clause: "no matching clause found"; not supplied: "not assessed" | "Within authority", with or without a quoted clause |

Body target: about 450 words excluding the source quotes appendix. When trimming, move fragments to the appendix first, then shorten Context. Never trim a dissent row.

## Signal phrases

These help locate passages; the fragment itself decides the classification, not the phrase.

| Looking for | Typical phrases | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Decision taken | "agreed", "we'll go with", "the decision is", "let's proceed", "approved", a chair's summary, a vote result | "Let's proceed" from one participant is not a body's decision unless nobody objects and the chair or forum confirms; otherwise status NOT TAKEN, quote the fragment, and list the proposal under Conditions and open points with who raised it |
| Condition | "subject to", "provided that", "as long as", "once we have" | Quote the condition in full; a condition is part of the decision |
| Deferral | "park this", "come back to it", "next meeting", "when we have the numbers" | Record the date or trigger stated; none stated: UNKNOWN |
| Escalation | "take it to", "needs sign-off from", "not ours to decide" | Name the body exactly as spoken |
| Dissent | "I'm not comfortable", "I disagree", "for the record", "I'd push back", "I abstain", "noted but" | A question is not dissent unless the speaker frames it as an objection; record it under open points instead |
| Owner | "you own this", "I'll take it", "who's picking this up", a name plus a verb | "Someone should" names nobody: owner UNKNOWN |
| Dates | "by Friday", "end of quarter", "before the audit" | Resolve against the discussion date; a trigger without a date is recorded as the trigger |

## Fragment rules

- A fragment is copied verbatim, up to 25 words, with a location (timestamp, message number, page or line).
- Speaker attribution comes from an explicit label or an explicit self-reference in the text; anything else is "unattributed".
- When a figure is corrected later in the discussion, both values enter the evidence table with the correcting fragment.
- A fragment used in the body appears in the source quotes appendix with the section that used it.
