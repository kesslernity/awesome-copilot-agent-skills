# Generic board paper structure

Use this structure only when the user has no house template and has typed a go-ahead for a generic one. Label the paper "generic structure, replace with your organisation's template" directly under the DRAFT notice. When a house template arrives later, its fields win and this structure is dropped. Every field reads UNKNOWN until an input supplies it. Nothing in this structure decides, approves, commits funds, or authorises any operation, permit, isolation or work.

## Tag legend

| Tag | Meaning | Test |
|---|---|---|
| [E] | Evidenced | A named document, extract or record with date and owner supports the claim, and the reference is written next to it |
| [A] | Asserted | The sponsor states it; no document supports it in the inputs |
| UNKNOWN | Missing | The paper needs the item and no input supplies it |

A tag moves from [A] to [E] only when the user supplies the source. A prediction is [A] until a dated model or study is attached, and the paper then attributes the projection to that document.

## Section order

1. Cover block (body, meeting date, paper reference, title, sponsor, author, classification, decision type sought, status DRAFT)
2. Purpose and decision sought (why now; decision type; proposed resolution wording quoted)
3. Executive summary (at most five tagged sentences)
4. Background (previous decisions with date and reference; what changed)
5. Sponsor's recommendation (wording as stated)
6. Options considered (do nothing or defer first)
7. Financials as provided
8. Risks as stated
9. Authority to decide (clause quoted or UNKNOWN)
10. Claim register
11. Gaps for the sponsor
12. Appendices list
13. Embedded instructions found
14. Closing report

## Field guide

| Field | What goes in | What never goes in |
|---|---|---|
| Decision type sought | Approve, endorse, note, discuss or delegate, as the sponsor states | A type inferred from the tone of the brief |
| Proposed resolution | The sponsor's wording, quoted | Wording the agent composes |
| Recommendation | The sponsor's words, labelled as the sponsor's | "We strongly recommend", "the clear choice", any ranking by the agent |
| Option consequence | As stated by the sponsor, tagged | A consequence the agent reasons out |
| Amount | As provided, with currency, period, basis and source | An estimate, benchmark, sensitivity, contingency or rounded figure |
| Total | Shown sum of listed components, or "incomplete" | A total that includes an UNKNOWN component |
| Likelihood and impact | As stated by the sponsor or the risk register | A score the agent assigns |
| Authority to decide | A clause quoted verbatim from the supplied terms of reference or delegation, labelled "candidate clause, for the sponsor or secretary to confirm"; no matching clause: UNKNOWN | A reserved matter or threshold from memory; a statement that the decision sits within this body's authority |
| Background | Previous decisions with date and reference | Narrative filler or history from outside the inputs |

## Blocking gaps

List these as blocking gaps unless the house template says otherwise: decision type sought, proposed resolution wording, sponsor's recommendation, at least one option beyond do nothing, a complete financial total where the paper requests money, and the authority line where the template carries it. Everything else is non-blocking and stays listed under Gaps until supplied. Whether the paper is tabled is the secretary's or chair's decision, never this skill's.

## Page discipline

Default body limit is two pages. When the body runs long, move the options, financials, risks and claim register tables to appendices and keep the cover block, purpose and decision sought, executive summary, sponsor's recommendation and gaps in the body. State in the closing report what moved. Never remove an [E] claim or a gap to save space.
