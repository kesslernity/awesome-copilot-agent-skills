# Scoring basis guide

Method for turning a supplier's evidence into a provisional score that the panel can trace and moderate. The plan supplies the criteria, weights, scale and anchors; this guide supplies only the way they are applied.

## Anchor matching

For each criterion and supplier:
1. Gather every passage in the response that bears on the criterion, with its location (section, page, question number). A later dated clarification answer supersedes the response on that point where the plan's clarification rule allows it; keep both, and where the plan has no rule put the change on the moderation list for the panel's admissibility ruling.
2. Read the anchors from the lowest level up. The score is the highest level whose wording the evidence satisfies in full. A passage that satisfies part of a level does not reach it.
3. Record the shortest quote that shows the level was reached, and its location. If two passages are needed, quote both.
4. Assign the confidence label (below). If the label is Asserted, apply the plan's rule for unsupported claims; if the plan has none, score against the anchors as written and flag the cell "Asserted: panel to confirm".

Never score on tone, length, presentation or the agent's impression of the supplier. Never score on knowledge the response does not contain.

## Confidence labels

- Evidenced: the response offers a fact, figure, named document, demonstration, reference or test result that can be checked.
- Asserted: the response claims the point without anything checkable ("fully supports", "market leading", "will comply").
- Partial: the evidence covers part of the criterion; name the part not covered.
- Not scored: no passage addresses the criterion. Carries no number.
- Verify against source: part of the response was unreadable; the cell needs a human read.

## Missing answers

Default rule when the plan is silent: a missing answer is Not scored, is excluded from the total, and the supplier's total is marked "Partial (n not scored)". A missing answer becomes zero only when the plan states that rule, and then the cell reads "0 (plan rule: missing = 0)".

Every Not scored and Partial cell goes into the missing answers register with a proposed clarification question. The question asks for the fact the plan wanted ("State the recovery time objective offered for the production service and attach the evidence.") and never for the answer that would score well.

## Arithmetic

Weighted score = raw score divided by scale maximum, multiplied by the criterion weight. Keep full precision through the calculation; round once, at the total, to one decimal place unless the plan states otherwise.

Worked example, scale 0 to 4, weight 20:
- Supplier A raw 3: 3 / 4 x 20 = 15.0
- Supplier B raw 2: 2 / 4 x 20 = 10.0
- Supplier C Not scored: no weighted value; total marked Partial (1 not scored)

Provisional total = sum of weighted scores over the criteria that carry a number. Show the sum, the count of criteria included, and the status. A weight total that does not equal the plan's stated total is reported and held; it is never renormalised by the agent.

## Price

Score price only by the formula the plan states, and show every step (for example, lowest price divided by this price, multiplied by the maximum score, then weighted). With no formula, list each price element as stated with its location, with no score and no comparative label.

## Calibration

After all cells are scored, compare cells criterion by criterion across suppliers. Two cells resting on substantially the same evidence must carry the same score. For each pair that does not:
- quote both passages,
- state both scores,
- align them to the level the anchors support and say so, or, where the evidence differs in a way the anchors do not settle, leave both and write "panel to decide".

Calibration differences are shown in their own table; they are never smoothed out silently.

## Sensitivity

For each criterion, ask: if one supplier's raw score on this criterion moved one level up or down, would the order of provisional totals change? List each case with the criterion, the supplier, the change, the resulting order and the margin between the adjacent totals before the change. Offer no view on which change is likely or deserved.

## Gates

A gate is pass or fail in the plan and is never weighted. The matrix reports each gate cell as Evidenced (with quote and location), Not evidenced (with the nearest passage or "No answer") or UNKNOWN. The words disqualified, failed, excluded, compliant and non-compliant do not appear; the panel rules on gates. Scoring continues for every supplier so the panel sees the whole picture.

## Vocabulary that never appears

Winner, preferred, recommended, award, shortlist, best value, cheapest, strongest, lowest risk, disqualified, compliant, non-compliant, approved, selected. The matrix reports provisional scores, their basis, their gaps and their arithmetic. The panel moderates, decides and records its own scores under the organisation's procurement governance. Nothing in the matrix authorises a purchase, a contract, operations, permits, isolations or work.
