# Example brief

Fictional example, for formatting and density reference only. Every role, vendor and detail below is invented. Attendees are shown by role here; a real brief carries the names from the invite. Match the structure, bullet length and tone; never copy the content into a real brief.

---

DRAFT meeting prep: Q3 vendor renewal review, 2026-06-12

2026-06-12, 14:00 to 14:45, online, 5 attendees, organised by the procurement lead.

**Meeting purpose**

Decide whether to renew the incumbent support contract at the quoted 12% uplift or move to the two-vendor split the procurement lead proposed. A good outcome is a renewal decision plus an owner for the negotiation. (inferred)

**Attendees and what each likely wants**

- **Procurement lead (organiser):** approval for the two-vendor split pitched in Monday's thread.
- **Finance partner:** the uplift held under 8%, per the 5 June email.
- **Service owner:** continuity guarantees before any vendor change.
- **Vendor account manager (external):** renewal signed before quarter end.
- **Legal counsel:** No recent signal.

**Open threads and unresolved questions**

- Renewal pricing thread: finance partner asked you on 5 June for last year's incident costs, still unanswered.
- Two-vendor split: service owner questioned migration risk in chat on 8 June, no reply from procurement.
- Contract end date: vendor says 31 July, the draft contract says 15 August, unconfirmed.

**Relevant files**

- **renewal-quote-v3.pdf** (vendor account manager, 6 June): quoted pricing with the 12% uplift and new service tiers.
- **vendor-split-proposal.docx** (procurement lead, 8 June): the two-vendor model with cost comparison.
- **fy26-support-incidents.xlsx** (service owner, 4 June): incident volumes the finance partner wants costed.

**Suggested talking points**

- Bring the incident cost answer finance is waiting on, or commit to a date.
- Ask procurement to address the migration risk question before a decision.
- Confirm the real contract end date before agreeing any timeline.

**Risks or tensions to be aware of**

- Procurement and the service owner disagreed openly in chat on 8 June about migration risk.
- The vendor's quarter-end push may rush a decision finance has not signed off.
